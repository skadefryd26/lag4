What Green, Yellow and Red mean

Rule Engine assigns every claim one of three outcomes. These same three outcomes appear at both the Decision & Routing step and again at Payment & Settlement.

Green Claims

Automated Resolution

Claim characteristics

Simple, predictable, low-risk. We have all the information we need — the claim continues through automation without a handler.

Yellow Claims

Requires Manual Handling

Claim characteristics

Requires clarification, judgement or coordination — routed to a claims handler through Sofie (manual claims handling).

Red Claims

Automated Decline

Claim characteristics

Claims outside coverage — declined automatically. Some Red cases in Norway can still route to manual handling.

How Rule Engine routing differs

Rule Engine evaluates every claim and returns Green (continue automatically), Yellow (manual handling required) or Red (rejected) — but what happens next isn't identical in both countries:

Norway

Yellow cases — and some Red cases — enter manual claims handling. Green cases and some standard Red cases continue directly to Slow Chat Message Template, archived in eArkiv and communicated via Claims Follow and Claims Communication.

Denmark

Yellow cases enter manual claims handling. Green and Red cases continue to IDIT for further processing, with the result communicated to the customer through Claims Communication.
