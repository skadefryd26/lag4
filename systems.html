Systems Catalogue

The full claims systems reference — split into three layers by who uses each system: customers, claims handlers (Sofie), and the backend/core platforms behind the scenes. Each system includes what it's used for, where it fits in the journey, and whether it's Norway-specific, Denmark-specific, or shared.

These are the systems customers directly interact with throughout the claims journey — to report a claim, provide information, book repairs or treatment, register expenses, receive decisions and compensation, and follow claim status.
